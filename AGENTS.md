# Project operating rules

- Communicate with the user in Persian. Use English filenames, identifiers, and technical configuration.
- Read `docs/product-brief.md`, `docs/decisions.md`, the constitution, and the relevant feature specification before implementation.
- Spec Kit is the single owner of specification-driven development. Do not introduce a competing planning, task, or document workflow.
- Use the relevant installed skill for each substantive work category. Keep work inside the current authorized phase.
- Treat approved decisions, proposed defaults, assumptions, and open questions as distinct states. Do not silently promote one into another.
- Keep changes lean and scoped. Record consequential decisions and update every affected specification.
- Scoring must be deterministic, specified, explainable, and testable. An LLM must not invent authoritative scores, product facts, or current prices.
- Test meaningful behavior, boundaries, permissions, validation, and failure cases. Interactive or mock checks do not replace repeatable tests.
- Never claim a check passed unless it ran and its output was inspected. Never present mock AI behavior as a live-provider result.
- Preserve privacy, accessible Persian/RTL behavior, low operational cost, and maintainable single-repository architecture.
